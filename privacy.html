MailTop Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

Short version: MailTop runs 100% locally inside your browser to reverse Gmail conversation display order. We never read, store, transmit, or sell your emails, Gmail contacts, or browsing activity.

1. Permissions & Local Operation

  • Gmail Layout Access (https://mail.google.com/*): Used strictly to apply local CSS ordering and lightweight layout attributes to Gmail thread views. Email content is never collected, logged, or transmitted off your device.
  • Local Storage (storage): Used strictly to remember your toggle preferences and optional Pro license activation state inside chrome.storage.local.

2. Optional Pro License Verification

If you purchase MailTop Pro and enter a license key, the extension sends only the license key and our public Polar.sh organization identifier to Polar's public license validation API (https://api.polar.sh/v1/customer-portal/license-keys/) to confirm whether the key is active. No email content or personal data is included.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

MailTop contains no ads, no tracking scripts, and no analytics SDKs. Optional Pro purchases are processed securely by Polar.sh as Merchant of Record.

4. Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact us at eray.tufekci@hotmail.com or via our GitHub page.
